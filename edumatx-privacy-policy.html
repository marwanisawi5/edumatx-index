The Service Provider retains personal data based on its necessity for the stated purposes:

Account & Data Deletion

EduMatX provides users with full control over their account records and personal data. You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal information at any time.

Account Deletion Procedures by Role:

  • Teachers: Can immediately delete their account at any time directly within the application via Profile & Settings > Delete Account, or by submitting an email request below. Deleting a Teacher account permanently purges the teacher's profile and disassociates linked class records.
  • Students & Assistants: Because student and assistant accounts are provisioned within an academic classroom context, deletion requests can be initiated in-app via Profile & Settings > Request Account Deletion. This submits a request to the assigned Teacher for review and confirmation. Alternatively, requests can be submitted directly via email below.
  • Parents: Parent accounts are view-only profiles linked to student profiles. They do not store independent personal data and are automatically deleted when the associated student account is permanently erased.

Deletion Requests When App is Uninstalled:

If you have uninstalled the Application or are unable to access your in-app settings, you can request account and personal data deletion by contacting the Service Provider directly at marwan.amr.isawi@outlook.com. Please provide your registered identifier/username, full name, and assigned role. Upon verification of your identity, all authentication credentials, personal names, and push notification tokens stored across our backend systems (Supabase and Firebase) will be permanently purged within 14 business days.
